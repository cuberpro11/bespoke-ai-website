Bespoke vs. subscription

Stop renting software built for someone else’s business.

Off-the-shelf automation tools bill per user, per workflow, and per renewal. Every new hire or added process raises the price, while you bend your operations around features designed for a generic customer. Bespoke software is built once around how your firm runs, with no subscription meter and no workarounds.

  • 01

    No per-user or subscription fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per seat, per user, or per automation run. Scale your team and your workflows without scaling your software bill. In the long run, bespoke wins.

  • 02

    Exactly the workflow you need

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project to map how your team actually works, so you get exactly what you want rather than a tool you’re only partially satisfied with.

  • 03

    You own every line

    The code, integrations, and data are yours. No vendor can raise prices, sunset a feature, or lock your documents behind an export fee.

  • 04

    Support that doesn’t end at launch

    Our engineers stay with you after go-live to handle support challenges, build new features, and respond fast when something urgent comes up.

  • 05

    Branded as your firm

    Portals, emails, and documents carry your firm’s name, logo, and colors, so clients experience your brand from end to end.
