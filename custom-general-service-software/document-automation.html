    Bespoke vs. subscription

    Document assembly shouldn’t cost more every time you draft.

    Document automation platforms charge per user, per template, or per generated document, so the more agreements you produce, the more you pay. A bespoke drafting engine is built once around your templates and clause logic, and generates as many documents as you need at no added cost.

    • 01

      No per-user or per-document fees

      Our pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per seat, per template, or per document generated. Draft more without paying more. Bespoke AI is more affordable in the long run.

    • 02

      Exactly your templates and logic

      A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your drafters and reviewers to capture every template, clause rule, and approval step you rely on.

    • 03

      You own the templates

      Your source code, clause libraries, and generated documents stay with you. Nothing is trapped in a proprietary format you can’t take with you.

    • 04

      Support as your documents change

      We stay on after launch to handle support issues, add new templates and document types, and respond to emergencies when a deal is closing.

    • 05

      Branded documents

      Generated documents, portals, and e-sign flows carry your letterhead, logo, and colors, so every output looks like your firm produced it.
