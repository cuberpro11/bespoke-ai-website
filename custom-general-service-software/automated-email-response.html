Bespoke vs. subscription

Inbox automation without a per-mailbox bill.

Help desk and email automation tools charge per agent, per mailbox, and per AI resolution, so a busier inbox becomes a more expensive one. A bespoke email response engine is built once around your firm’s tone and routing rules, with no per-seat or per-message pricing.

  • 01

    No per-agent or per-message fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per mailbox, per agent, or per AI reply. Handle twice the volume without twice the bill. Bespoke is more affordable over time.

  • 02

    Written in your voice

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your team to define routing rules, review queues, and reply templates that sound exactly like your firm.

  • 03

    You own the engine

    Your code, prompts, and correspondence history stay in your environment, never on a shared vendor platform that could change its terms or access.

  • 04

    Support after go-live

    We stay on after launch to handle support issues, tune responses, add new inboxes and workflows, and respond to emergencies quickly.

  • 05

    Branded on every reply

    Every automated reply, signature, and client portal carries your firm’s name and brand, so clients never see a third-party tool.
