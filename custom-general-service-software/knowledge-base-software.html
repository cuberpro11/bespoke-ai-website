    Bespoke vs. subscription

    Your firm’s knowledge shouldn’t sit behind a per-seat license.

    Enterprise search and knowledge tools charge per user, so every employee who needs answers adds to the bill, and AI features are often sold as costly add-ons. A bespoke knowledge base is built once over your own documents, so the whole firm can use it without a per-seat meter.

    1. 01

      No per-seat fees

      Our pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per user or per AI query. Give every employee access without multiplying the cost. Bespoke AI is the more affordable option over time.

    2. 02

      Organized the way you work

      A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your team to structure collections, permissions, and search exactly the way your people look for answers.

    3. 03

      You own your knowledge

      Your documents, search index, and source code stay in your environment. Your institutional knowledge never becomes a vendor’s asset.

    4. 04

      Support as your library grows

      We stay on after launch to handle support issues, connect new document sources, add features, and respond to emergencies quickly.

    5. 05

      Branded for your firm

      The knowledge portal carries your firm’s name, logo, and colors, so it feels like an internal tool your team owns.
