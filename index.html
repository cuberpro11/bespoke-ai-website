Bespoke vs. subscription

Software you own beats software you rent forever.

Subscription platforms charge per seat, per user, and per renewal, so the bill grows every time your team does, for a product built for everyone and fitted to no one. Bespoke software flips that math: one engineering investment, no licensing meter, and a platform shaped around exactly how your firm works.

  • 01

    No per-seat or subscription fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per seat or per user. You invest once in engineering instead of paying a licensing bill that compounds every year, so bespoke is more affordable in the long run.

  • 02

    Exactly what you asked for

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project to map your workflows and screens with your team. You get exactly what you want, not a tool you’re only partially happy with.

  • 03

    You own it

    The source code, the data, and the roadmap belong to you. Your software becomes a permanent asset on your balance sheet instead of a recurring line item you can never stop paying.

  • 04

    We stay after launch

    We don’t hand over the keys and disappear. Our team stays on to handle support challenges, build additional features, and respond to emergencies long after go-live.

  • 05

    Branded as yours

    Every screen carries your firm’s name, logo, colors, and domain. Your clients and staff see your brand, not a vendor’s.
