SpecificationBespoke platform for owners, brokerages & property managers

Bespoke custom property management software development

  • Move beyond off-the-shelf software limitations with custom property management software designed around your specific leasing workflows, maintenance routing, and rent collection logic.
  • Build integrated tenant portals, automated lease renewal engines, and vendor dispatch modules that reduce administrative overhead across portfolios.

Lease abstraction & document data extraction

  • Automatically parse complex commercial leases, amendment addendums, and offering memorandums to extract key escalation terms, rent schedules, and termination options.
  • Feed extracted lease terms directly into core accounting and asset management engines with zero manual data entry.

Automated deal flow & underwriting pipelines

  • Centralize property acquisition tracking, rent roll analysis, and underwriting models within a unified investment dashboard.
  • Ingest unstructured property listings, tax records, and operating statements to calculate NOI, cap rates, and cash-on-cash returns automatically.

Private RAG document search for asset management

  • Securely query your entire repository of title insurance policies, environmental reports, and lender agreements using natural language.
  • Provide asset managers with immediate, verifiable citations from legal agreements while keeping proprietary deal data completely private.
