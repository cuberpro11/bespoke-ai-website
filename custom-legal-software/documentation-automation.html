Data security & confidentiality architecture

Bank-grade security and closed AI-loops for law firms.

Standard public AI tools carry immense data privacy risks, and uploading confidential documents to them is often a breach of attorney-client privilege. Our legal software is built with complete data isolation from day one to give your firm the power of AI without the legal complications.

Closed AI-loops (zero public model training)

  • When an attorney enters client data, case strategy, or proprietary contract clauses into your system, that data stays completely locked within your firm.
  • Your information is never sent out to be used for public AI training, nor can it be accessed or indexed by third-party LLM providers.

SOC 2 compliant standards

  • Every system we build follows strict SOC 2 compliance guidelines for data security, availability, and processing integrity.
  • Implement role-based access controls (RBAC) to ensure sensitive client files are only accessible to authorized team members.

AWS infrastructure & enterprise encryption

  • Deployed inside isolated Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud environments using AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
  • Maintain total control over your data residency, automated backup schedules, and private cloud parameters. Bespoke AI will never own, nor claim ownership of, your firm's data.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Contract automation without seat-based legal tech pricing.

Legal drafting platforms charge exorbitant per-attorney rates, plus premiums for clause libraries and AI features, so a firm pays more every time it adds a lawyer. Bespoke contract automation is built once around your precedents and playbooks, with no seat-based meter.

  • 01

    No per-attorney fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per attorney, per seat, or per agreement generated. Draft at volume without paying at volume. Bespoke is more affordable over time.
