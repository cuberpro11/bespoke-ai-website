Document & data automation

Risk management documentation automation via AI.

Risk officers spend hundreds of hours manually reviewing, extracting, and cross-referencing data across complex loan applications, regulatory disclosures, audit reports, and counterparty filings. We engineer bespoke document automation engines that transform unstructured compliance documents into structured, real-time risk intelligence without compromising on data privacy.

Automated credit & counterparty risk extraction

  • Automatically parse audited financial statements, credit memos, tax filings, and 10-K/10-Q disclosures.
  • Extract key financial ratios, debt covenants, and liquidity metrics directly into your risk engines with zero manual data entry.

Regulatory compliance & policy mapping

  • Continuously map regulatory policy updates (such as Basel III/IV, SEC mandates, or internal credit policies) directly against active client portfolios and loans.
  • Leverage AI and risk management automation tools to auto-flag non-compliant terms, covenant breaches, or high-risk exposure concentrations.

Audit trail & regulatory filing generation

  • Convert complex internal risk analysis and stress-test models into standardized, audit-ready compliance reports in seconds.
  • Draft comprehensive credit risk memos and regulatory disclosures tailored strictly to your firm's governance standards.

Private RAG risk vault search

  • Securely query your firm's entire repository of historical loan files, legal contracts, counterparty agreements, and policy guidelines using natural language.
  • Provide risk analysts with immediate, verifiable citations from internal files while ensuring sensitive financial data is never exposed to public LLMs.
