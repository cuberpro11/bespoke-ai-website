Solutions / Finance / Trading

Custom Trading Platform Development

Execute proprietary strategies with zero latency compromise. We engineer high-frequency execution engines, custom algorithmic dashboards, and broker-neutral trading infrastructure built for hedge funds, prop desks, and asset managers.

Core capabilities & data automation

Custom trading software for algorithmic & institutional workflows.

Proprietary funds and asset managers spend hundreds of hours attempting to bend off-the-shelf trading terminals to fit custom execution strategies and risk models. We deliver full-stack custom trading software development tailored specifically around your firm's unique alpha generation engines, order routing logic, and data storage needs. That work covers Foreign Exchange (Forex), Algorithmic Trading (Algo), and cryptocurrencies.

Stages 01–02 · Signal & risk

Algorithmic strategy integration & signal engines

  • Embed custom quantitative models, automated signal triggers, and algorithmic rebalancing rules directly into your trading engine.
  • Combine real-time tick streaming with automated risk checks to ensure trade execution strictly adheres to firm drawdown limits and position sizing rules.
Stages 03–04 · Router & venues

High-performance execution & broker-neutral connectivity

  • Specialized custom trading software designed to connect natively to multiple liquidity providers, exchanges, and prime brokers via FIX protocol and REST/WebSocket APIs.
  • Eliminate platform dependency by building a unified order management system (OMS) and execution management system (EMS) with microsecond latency controls.
Stages 05–06 · Fills & PnL

Real-time portfolio risk & analytics dashboards

  • Monitor real-time PnL, Value at Risk (VaR), portfolio exposure, and margin utilization across fragmented market venues in a single consolidated interface.
  • Streamline post-trade settlement processing and automated trade reporting with direct database connections to back-office accounting tools.
Beneath every stage · the feed

Private data pipelines & machine learning models

  • Securely ingest, normalize, and process unstructured alternative market data, news sentiment feeds, and historical tick data using private data architecture.
  • Deploy custom AI models to surface market regime shifts and liquidity imbalances without exposing proprietary signal logic to third-party SaaS vendors.

Foreign Exchange (Forex)

Custom trading software for automating Foreign Exchange (Forex) trades

Desks with their own FX workflow need software shaped around that book. We build custom trading software for automating Foreign Exchange (Forex) trades inside isolated Amazon Web Services (AWS) environments, engineered as SOC 2 compliant software so order flow, positions, and counterparty data stay inside the firm.

Foreign Exchange (Forex) news apps

  • Bespoke AI builds custom Foreign Exchange (Forex) news apps that pull wires, central-bank releases, and desk commentary into a private feed your traders use.
  • Headlines stay on your infrastructure, mapped to the pairs and sessions the desk actually watches.

Foreign Exchange (Forex) applications for PC environments

  • Foreign Exchange (Forex) applications for PC environments put execution, blotters, and charting on the desk workstation.
  • Each desktop client wires into the firm's own order and market-data stack, with the layout the traders already work in.

AWS hosting and SOC 2 controls

  • Security is part of the build. We host on Amazon Web Services (AWS) with encryption at rest and in transit.
  • SOC 2 compliant software uses role-based access so strategy and client flow stay inside the firm. Bespoke AI will never own, nor claim ownership of, your firm's data.

Algorithmic Trading (Algo)

Custom high frequency trading application

A custom high frequency trading application keeps signal, risk, and routing on infrastructure you control. We engineer Algorithmic Trading (Algo) systems for listed markets and for cryptocurrencies, hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and built as SOC 2 compliant software.

Custom algorithmic trading application for cryptocurrencies

  • A custom algorithmic trading application for cryptocurrencies connects your models to exchange and OTC liquidity.
  • Pre-trade checks and position limits run before an order leaves the firm, on the same clock as the strategy.

Bespoke algorithmic trading platform for cryptocurrencies

  • A bespoke algorithmic trading platform for cryptocurrencies is one place to run strategies, watch inventory, and reconcile fills across venues.
  • The platform stays on your accounts and your keys, with signal logic held in the firm's own environment.

Private pipelines on AWS

  • Security for execution systems means private pipelines inside Amazon Web Services (AWS), with access limited to the people who run the book.
  • SOC 2 compliant software keeps proprietary signal logic off third-party training sets. The firm keeps the models and the data.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Own your execution stack instead of renting it.

Trading platforms and terminals are licensed per seat, per venue, and per data feed, and every added trader or strategy raises the monthly bill. A bespoke trading platform is engineered once for your strategies and infrastructure, so you scale desks and volume without scaling license fees.

  • 01

    No per-seat terminal fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, with no per-trader, per-seat, or subscription fees on the platform we build. Add traders and strategies without adding licenses. Over time, bespoke is the cheaper stack.

  • 02

    Built for your strategies

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your traders and quants to design exactly the order entry, blotters, and dashboards your desk needs.

  • 03

    Your edge stays yours

    You own the source code, execution logic, and strategy data outright. Your proprietary edge never lives on a vendor’s servers or depends on their roadmap.

  • 04

    Support at market speed

    We stay on after launch to handle support issues, add venues and features, and respond to emergencies immediately, because downtime during market hours is never acceptable.

  • 05

    Branded for your firm

    Client-facing portals and reporting carry your firm’s brand, presenting a polished, proprietary platform to investors and counterparties.
