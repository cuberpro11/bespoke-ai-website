Bespoke vs. subscription

Your platform shouldn’t charge you for every new analyst.

Financial software is sold the expensive way: per-seat licenses, per-user compliance modules, and renewal hikes that climb with your headcount. A bespoke platform is engineered once for your risk, compliance, and trading workflows, so your cost of ownership stays flat while your institution grows.

  • 01

    No per-seat licensing

    Our pricing is market competitive, with no per-seat, per-desk, or subscription fees. Add analysts, advisors, and desks without adding licenses. Over the life of the platform, bespoke is the more affordable path.

  • 02

    Fitted to your institution

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your traders, compliance officers, and operations staff. You get exactly the workflows and dashboards you need, not a vendor’s compromise.

  • 03

    You own the platform

    You own the source code, models, and data outright, with no vendor lock-in and nothing to migrate away from. Your platform is a proprietary asset, not a rented one.

  • 04

    Engineers on call after launch

    Markets don’t wait. We stay on after launch to resolve support issues, add features as regulations and strategies change, and respond to emergencies right away.

  • 05

    Branded for your institution

    Client portals, reports, and internal tools carry your institution’s brand, colors, and domain, reinforcing trust with every client interaction.
