Regulatory database software

Regulatory database software built around your obligations.

Off-the-shelf regulatory feeds hand your team thousands of rules and leave you to work out which ones apply. We build custom regulatory database software that ingests the rulebooks, guidance, and enforcement actions of the regulators you answer to, links every obligation to the policies and controls that satisfy it, and alerts the right owner the moment a rule changes.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Staying compliant shouldn’t cost more every renewal.

Regulatory compliance platforms are typically sold per user and per module, so every new compliance analyst, jurisdiction, or regulation adds to an annual bill that only goes up. Bespoke compliance software is built once around your obligations and controls, without a licensing meter attached to every reviewer.

  • CTL-01

    No per-user or per-module fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, with no per-seat licensing and no charge for adding a regulation or jurisdiction. Over the life of the platform, bespoke is the more affordable choice.

  • CTL-02

    Mapped to your obligations

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your compliance officers and legal team to model your exact controls, review workflows, and reporting requirements.

  • CTL-03

    You own the evidence

    Source code, policies, and audit evidence belong to you. Examiners see your system of record, not a vendor’s, and nothing is held hostage at contract renewal.

  • CTL-04

    On call when regulators are

    We stay on after launch to handle support issues, add features as rules change, and respond immediately to emergencies such as an exam request or audit deadline.

  • CTL-05

    Branded for your institution

    Attestation portals, reports, and internal dashboards carry your institution’s brand, so staff and examiners see one cohesive, professional system.
