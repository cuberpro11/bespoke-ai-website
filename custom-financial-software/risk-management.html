Bespoke vs. subscription

Risk analytics without a licensing meter on every desk.

Enterprise risk platforms are priced per user, per desk, and per data module, so extending coverage to a new strategy or asset class means another licensing negotiation. Bespoke risk management software is built once around your models and exposures, so you can extend it freely without the bill compounding.

R-01No per-user or per-desk feesOur pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per seat, per desk, or per data module. Extend risk coverage across new strategies and teams without paying again. In the long run, bespoke wins.
