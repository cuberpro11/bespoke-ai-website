Wealth Management AI Solutions

Bespoke AI is a premier custom wealth management software vendor working at the intersection of AI and wealth management. We empower advisors, streamline portfolio operations, and scale high-touch client relationships with private, enterprise-grade AI pipelines built for RIAs, private banks, and family offices, with no per-advisor or per-seat subscription fees.

Core capabilities & data automation

Document and data pipelines built for the advisory desk.

Portfolio managers spend hundreds of hours manually reviewing, extracting, and assembling data across client onboarding packets, tax records, and quarterly financial statements. Rather than reselling a one-size-fits-all product like a typical wealth management platform vendor, we engineer bespoke AI for wealth management: document automation engines that transform unstructured financial documents into structured, actionable insights without compromising on data privacy.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Grow the book without a fee on every advisor.

The typical wealth management platform vendor prices per advisor seat or as a share of assets, so your software bill grows every time you hire an advisor or win a client. Bespoke wealth management software is engineered once around your service model, so growth goes to your bottom line instead of a vendor’s.

  1. p. 01

    No per-advisor fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, and we never charge per advisor, per seat, or by assets under management. Grow your book without growing your software bill. Bespoke AI is more affordable in the long run.

  2. p. 02

    Built around your service model

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your advisors and operations staff to design exactly the client reviews, onboarding, and reporting your firm delivers.

  3. p. 03

    You own your client data

    Your source code and client data stay with your firm. No platform vendor sits between you and your clients or holds your data at contract renewal.

  4. p. 04

    Support after launch

    We stay on after launch to handle support issues, add features as your offering grows, and respond to emergencies before clients ever notice.

  5. p. 05

    Branded for your firm

    Client portals, statements, and reports carry your firm’s logo, colors, and domain, so every client interaction reinforces your brand.
