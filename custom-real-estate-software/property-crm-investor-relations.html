Streamline agent contact pipelines, property marketing, syndication reporting, and investor relations with custom cloud software. All built without recurring annual subscriptions or per-seat fees.

Core operational pillars

01 / 03

Engineered as a custom property CRM system.

Managing property inquiries, client databases, and showing activity requires a lightweight, rapid-response interface. Legacy systems clutter day-to-day operations with unused administrative features, slowing down agent follow-ups.

  • Centralized Contact Intelligence: Leverage commercial real estate contact management software to track buyer preferences, deal histories, lease expiration dates, and custom contact tags in a single source of truth.
  • On-the-Go Mobile Execution: Built as a native mobile crm for real estate agents, allowing field teams to update deal status, log phone calls, send client texts, and capture leads directly from property sites.
  • Brokerage & Agency Pipelines: Configured as an intuitive estate agent crm software solution, providing visual drag-and-drop pipelines across active listings, client inquiries, and transaction milestones.

Bank-grade security & investor compliance

Institutional security for financial & capital records.

Private equity raises and high-value property transactions involve accredited investor documentation, wire details, and confidential tax filings. Every system engineered by Bespoke AI incorporates multi-layered security controls to ensure data protection.

  • SOC 2 Type II compliant architecture

    Investor records, banking details, and subscription agreements are protected using end-to-end encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit) across isolated cloud server environments.

  • Role-based access control (RBAC)

    Define granular permissions so deal sponsors, fund managers, broker-dealers, and external investors access only authorized investment data.

  • Immutable audit logging

    Maintain timestamped audit records for every document download, digital signature, capital call, and financial transaction across the platform.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Going bespoke beats legacy real estate CRM subscriptions.

Legacy CRM and investor portal SaaS vendors charge aggressive, per-seat or basis-point subscription models. Firms are forced to pay escalating recurring fees for rigid platforms that cannot adapt to unique fund waterfall structures or brokerage workflows. Bespoke AI offers a transparent, sustainable alternative to the predatory pricing structures that real estate and private equity executives are accustomed to.

  1. Predictable, Market-Competitive Capital Investment

    Market-competitive engineering pricing is provided with zero per-agent licensing costs, no basis-point assets-under-management (AUM) fees, and no recurring annual SaaS subscriptions. Firms pay once for custom engineering, keeping operational overhead flat regardless of investor network growth or deal volume. Organizations pay only standard recurring costs for private hosting, maintenance, or voluntary platform enhancements.

    01

  2. 100% Custom CRM & Investor Workflows (No Cookie-Cutter Templates)

    Every estate agency and private equity firm operates with distinct capital raising models, deal pipelines, and client communication workflows. A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project to collaborate directly with fund managers, brokers, and investor relations leads to build custom CRM interfaces, subscription portals, and distribution tracking engines matching exact operational strategy.

    02

  3. Full Intellectual Property & System Ownership

    Clients own the software and source code outright upon project completion. Real estate firms retain total control over LP databases, investor contact history, custom waterfall calculations, and platform logic thus converting software expenditures into permanent institutional assets on the corporate balance sheet.

    03

  4. Bespoke Corporate & Sponsor Branding

    Property CRM interfaces, client portals, and investor portals are fully custom-branded to align with official corporate design standards, logos, visual palettes, and custom domain structures. The result is a refined, high-touch digital experience for accredited investors and property clients.

    04

  5. Dedicated Engineering Support & Long-Term Partnership

    Software is never deployed and abandoned. Bespoke AI serves as a dedicated engineering partner by providing ongoing maintenance, emergency support during active capital raises or reporting cycles, and continuous feature updates as strategic fund goals evolve.
