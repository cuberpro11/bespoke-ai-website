Custom Real Estate CRMs & Mobile Texting Platforms

Empower agents, brokerages, and transaction coordinators with bespoke software designed for high-speed lead response, mobile communication, and automated closing workflows with zero recurring annual subscriptions or per-seat fees.

Core operational pillars

01 / 03

Custom real estate texting & assistant software.

Top-producing agents live in the field showing properties, meeting clients, and attending closings. When a prospect submits an inquiry through Zillow, Realtor.com, or your website, immediate response time is the difference between securing a listing or losing it to a competitor.

  • Instant SMS Lead Engagement: Automatically engage inbound portal leads via instant, personalized text messages the second an inquiry is submitted.
  • Unified Client Text Threads: Keep all buyer, seller, and vendor SMS history central and accessible across both mobile and web interfaces without relying on agents' personal, unmonitored texting apps.
  • AI-Assisted Field Responses: Leverage custom voice-to-text and AI assistant features that let agents trigger follow-up tasks, schedule showings, and draft client updates while on the road.

02 / 03

Engineered as a dedicated CRM for property agents.

Generic, off-the-shelf CRMs force agents into rigid workflows stuffed with bloated features that are often used seldomly. Our custom real estate assistant CRM solutions prioritize extreme speed, clean mobile UX, and actionable pipeline visibility.

  • Tailored Lead Routing: Automatically route incoming leads based on custom brokerage criteria, geographic zip codes, agent performance, or round-robin logic.
  • Zero-Clutter Visual Pipelines: Track prospects effortlessly across distinct transaction stages: New Lead → Property Showing → Offer Submitted → Under Contract → Closed.
  • Automated Follow-Up Sequences: Ensure no prospect slips through the cracks with automated SMS and email drips, task reminders, and automated inactivity alerts for sleeping leads.

03 / 03

Streamlined real estate closing software integration.

Taking a deal from mutual acceptance to key handover requires flawless coordination between buyers, sellers, lenders, title agents, and escrow officers. We build custom closing software that automates milestone tracking and reduces administrative friction.

  • Automated Closing Checklists: Eliminate manual tracking with milestone-driven checklists that assign tasks directly to agents, coordinators, and clients.
  • Document Management & Status Portals: Provide buyers and sellers with clean, branded client portals to upload disclosures, track appraisal progress, and view clear closing schedules.
  • Automated Vendor & Attorney Alerts: Instantly notify lenders, title companies, and home inspectors as critical contract contingencies are met.

Bank-grade security & compliance

Enterprise security built for sensitive real estate transactions.

Real estate transactions involve sensitive client financial records, identity verification documents, and high-value wire transfers. We engineer every custom CRM platform with multi-layered security protocols to safeguard your business and your clients.

  • SOC 2 Type II compliant architecture

    Built using end-to-end encrypted databases (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit) to protect client identity and financial documentation.

  • Role-based access controls (RBAC)

    Restrict sensitive client data, commission splits, and contract files so agents only access the records necessary for their active deals.

  • Wire fraud safeguards & audit logs

    Mitigate fraud risks with immutable, timestamped audit logs for every document view, task completion, and communication entry.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Software you own beats software you rent forever.

Legacy real estate SaaS vendors charge aggressive per-agent or per-seat subscription models, forcing real estate firms to pay escalating monthly fees for rigid platforms that cannot adapt to specialized team structures or proprietary lead-generation workflows. Bespoke AI offers a transparent, sustainable alternative to the predatory pricing structures that real estate professionals are accustomed to.

  • 01

    Predictable, Market-Competitive Capital Investment

    We provide market-competitive engineering pricing with zero per-agent licensing costs, no seat-based fee hikes, and no recurring annual SaaS subscriptions. Pay once for custom engineering thus keeping brokerage operating costs flat regardless of how many agents join your team or how many leads your CRM processes. Your firm only pays normal recurring costs for cloud hosting, maintenance, or voluntary future feature additions.

  • 02

    100% Custom Agent & Closing Workflows (No Cookie-Cutter Templates)

    When we say bespoke, we really mean bespoke. A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project to work alongside brokers, team leads, and transaction coordinators to design custom SMS messaging interfaces, automated lead-distribution logic, and closing tracking portals that match each firm’s exact operational strategy.

  • 03

    Full Intellectual Property & System Ownership

    Each brokerage owns the software and source code outright. You retain total control over client contact data, transaction history, system logic, and future platform enhancements thus converting software expenses into a permanent institutional asset on your balance sheet.

  • 04

    Bespoke Brokerage & Team Branding

    Custom real estate assistant CRMs and client closing portals are fully custom-branded to align with your brokerage's official visual identity, logo guidelines, colors, and custom domain structure. The goal is to establish an impressive, high-touch brand experience for buyers and sellers.

  • 05

    Dedicated Engineering Support & Long-Term Partnership

    We don't just deploy software and disappear. Bespoke AI remains your dedicated engineering partner by providing ongoing maintenance, emergency support during high-volume sales windows, and custom feature additions as your market expansion strategies evolve.
