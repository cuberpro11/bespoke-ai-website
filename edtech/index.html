Our EdTech engineering capabilities.

Educational institutions, private academies, and EdTech innovators frequently outgrow standard off-the-shelf platforms. We engineer specialized educational software tailored around your unique administrative models, pedagogical frameworks, and student engagement strategies.

Bespoke learning management systems (LMS)

  • We architect bespoke learning management systems engineered from the ground up to replace rigid, one-size-fits-all course templates.
  • Deliver custom student dashboards, flexible course structure engines, and tailored grading rubrics aligned directly with your institution's curriculum.

Student information systems (SIS) & operational portals

  • Modernize enrollment, attendance tracking, transcript generation, and tuition management with custom administrative portals.
  • Connect disparate administrative departments into a central hub, ensuring data flows securely between admissions, registrar, and faculty systems.

AI-integrated curriculum & lesson planning

  • Build custom tools that ingest core educational materials to automatically generate structured lesson plans, reading guides, and interactive study modules.
  • Differentiate learning materials for diverse student reading levels while aligning content directly to state and institutional standards.

Automated grading & assessment platforms

  • Eliminate administrative bottlenecks with AI-assisted evaluation tools for essays, short-answer assignments, and problem sets.
  • Provide instant, rubric-aligned feedback to students while maintaining complete faculty oversight and approval controls.

Private AI tutoring & interactive study assistants

  • Deploy safe, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) study assistants trained strictly on your institution's verified textbook library and course materials.
  • Offer 24/7 personalized student guidance and concept review in a secure environment where student data remains completely private.

Document & data automation

Streamlining educational infrastructure & workflow automation.

Back-office friction slows down faculty and administrative teams alike. We build custom integrations and document automation systems to keep internal school operations flowing seamlessly.

Automated student intake & record processing

  • Parse incoming application packets, prior transcripts, and immunization records to auto-populate student files in your core SIS.

Interoperability & API integration

  • Connect legacy databases with modern SaaS tools via custom LTI (Learning Tools Interoperability) and API architectures, ensuring data synchronizes smoothly across your tech stack.

Analytics & early warning dashboards

  • Aggregate assignment completion, engagement metrics, and quiz scores to automatically flag at-risk students and recommend targeted intervention strategies.
