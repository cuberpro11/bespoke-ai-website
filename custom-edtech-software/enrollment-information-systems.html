Matriculation

Hand every admitted student into the record system you already run.

An enrollment platform should never operate in an isolated silo. The moment an applicant accepts their offer, their profile and documentation must transition smoothly into an institution’s ongoing academic framework.

Our enrollment architectures integrate natively with student record keeping systems thus instantly converting matriculated prospective applicants into official student records without manual data re-entry or data loss.

FERPA & Privacy Protection Standards

All prospective student data, financial documentation, and internal evaluation notes are strictly safeguarded in full compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and state-level data privacy mandates.

Enterprise AWS Cloud Infrastructure

Hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) using encrypted multi-region S3 storage, automated point-in-time backups, isolated Virtual Private Clouds (VPC), and AWS KMS key management for maximum data protection.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Protect applicant confidentiality with strict permission tiers ensuring that external evaluators, financial aid officers, faculty interviewers, and administrative staff only access the specific data required for their evaluation phase.

Encrypted Pipelines & Immutable Audit Logging

All document uploads, status changes, and evaluation scores are transmitted via end-to-end encryption with comprehensive audit logging for internal compliance and external reporting.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Going bespoke beats legacy EdTech subscriptions.

Colleges and universities are currently being drained of valuable capital funds by legacy admissions SaaS vendors that charge aggressive, per-applicant or per-seat subscription models. Institutions are forced to pay escalating annual fees for rigid platforms that cannot adapt to changing admissions policies or specialized review criteria. Bespoke AI offers a transparent, sustainable alternative to the predatory pricing structures that higher education institutions are accustomed to.

  • 01

    Predictable, Market-Competitive Capital Investment

    We provide market-competitive engineering pricing with zero per-applicant processing fees, no seat licensing costs, and no recurring annual SaaS subscriptions. You pay once for custom engineering, keeping your admissions operating costs flat regardless of how many applications your university processes. Your institution only pays the normal recurring costs for system upkeep, maintenance, or additional features.

  • 02

    100% Custom Evaluation Workflows (No Cookie-Cutter Templates)

    When we say bespoke, we really mean bespoke. Every institution evaluates applicants differently. A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works in close partnership with your Deans of Admission, financial aid officers, and IT leaders to design custom scoring algorithms, review workflows, and applicant portals that match your exact recruitment strategy.

  • 03

    Full Intellectual Property & System Ownership

    Your institution owns the software and source code outright. You retain total control over applicant data, system logic, and future platform enhancements, converting software expenses into a permanent institutional asset on your balance sheet.

  • 04

    Bespoke Institutional Branding

    Your prospective applicant portal is fully custom-branded to align with your university’s official visual identity, logo guidelines, colors, and campus domain structure. The goal is to establish an impressive, high-touch brand experience for prospective students and their families.

  • 05

    Dedicated Engineering Support & Long-Term Partnership

    We don’t just deploy software and disappear. Bespoke AI remains your dedicated engineering partner by providing ongoing maintenance, emergency support during high-volume deadline windows, and custom feature additions as your admissions strategies evolve.
