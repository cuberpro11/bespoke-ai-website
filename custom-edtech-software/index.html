Bespoke vs. subscription

Going bespoke beats legacy EdTech subscriptions.

Higher education institutions are being drained of critical funds by legacy vendors charging per-student and per-seat subscription fees that inflate every year, for rigid platforms that rarely fit how a campus actually runs. A bespoke higher education information management system replaces that compounding overhead with one investment in a platform built around your institution.

  • 01

    No per-student or per-seat fees

    Our pricing is market competitive, with zero per-seat licensing and no per-student subscription costs. As enrollment grows, your software bill doesn’t, which makes bespoke the more affordable choice over time.

  • 02

    Built around your campus

    A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project and works with your registrars, faculty, and IT staff to map every form, workflow, and screen to your exact requirements.

  • 03

    Your institution owns it

    You retain complete control over your source code, institutional data, and platform roadmap, turning software spend into a permanent asset.

  • 04

    A long-term engineering partner

    We stay on after launch to handle maintenance, emergency support during registration peaks, and new features as your institution grows.

  • 05

    Your institution’s brand

    Portals for students, faculty, and families carry your institution’s visual identity, colors, typography, and campus domain.
