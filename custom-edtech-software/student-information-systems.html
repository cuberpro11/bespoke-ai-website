Custom student information systems

Security & compliance

Enterprise-grade cloud security and data compliance.

Student records contain highly sensitive personal, financial, and academic information that demands non-negotiable security protocols. Bespoke AI frequently works with educational institutions to build EdTech systems with defensive, multi-layered cloud architectures designed to meet, or even exceed, strict regulatory mandates.

FERPA Compliance Standards

All data storage, transport, and access control models are strictly engineered to comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), ensuring student privacy and institutional legal compliance.

Amazon Web Services (AWS) Infrastructure

We leverage enterprise AWS cloud architecture by utilizing encrypted multi-region S3 storage, automated database backups, isolated Virtual Private Clouds (VPC), and AWS KMS for key management.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Granular permission structures guarantee that staff, faculty, administrative leaders, and students only access data required for their designated roles.

Zero-Trust Security & Audit Trails

Every database query, record modification, and export request is logged in immutable audit trails with real-time threat detection and encrypted end-to-end data pipelines.

Bespoke vs. subscription

Going bespoke beats legacy EdTech subscriptions.

Higher education institutions are currently being drained of critical capital funds by legacy software vendors enforcing outrageously expensive, subscription-based licensing models. As student enrollments fluctuate, universities are penalized with inflating annual fees for rigid, off-the-shelf software platforms that rarely fit their exact administrative requirements. Bespoke AI offers a transparent, sustainable alternative to the predatory pricing structures that higher education institutions are accustomed to.

  • 01

    Predictable, Market-Competitive Capital Investment

    We offer market-competitive initial engineering pricing with zero per-seat licensing fees and no ongoing per-student subscription costs. Pay once for custom engineering rather than committing your institution to compounding annual SaaS licensing overhead year after year. We often work with educational institutions to rebuild antiquated, and often highly expensive, legacy systems to reduce annual software expenses.

  • 02

    100% Tailored Engineering (No Cookie-Cutter Templates)

    When we say bespoke, we really mean bespoke. A User Experience Designer is assigned to every project to work in close partnership with your institution’s leadership, IT staff, registrars, and faculty to map every workflow, form, and interface to your exact operational specifications.

  • 03

    Full Intellectual Property & System Ownership

    When your platform is built, your institution owns the software outright. You retain complete control over your source code, institutional data, and platform roadmap, thus turning software expenditures into a permanent proprietary asset on your balance sheet.

  • 04

    Unique Institutional Branding

    Your platform is fully designed and custom-branded to reflect your institution’s official visual identity, colors, typography, and campus domain structure. We deliver professional, cohesive digital environments for students and faculty alike.

  • 05

    Dedicated Long-Term Support & Engineering Partnership

    We don’t launch your software and walk away. Bespoke AI remains your dedicated engineering team to handle system maintenance, emergency support, feature expansions, and technical scaling as your institution grows.
